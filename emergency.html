Triage Guide

When to go to the ER?

  • Chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Weakness/numbness on one side
  • Slurred speech
  • Fainting or change in mental state
  • Serious burns or deep cuts
  • Head or eye injury
  • Severe allergic reactions

*If you are experiencing any of these, call the hotline immediately or proceed to the nearest Emergency Room.

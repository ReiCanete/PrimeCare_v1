Password Reset Assistance

For the security of patient medical records and hospital operations, account password resets are verified by our support team:

Patients: Please contact the Admissions & Patient Desk or call Hospital Support with your Patient ID and a valid government-issued ID.

Staff & Clinicians: Please contact Hospital IT Support to request a temporary password reset.

Already signed in? Patients can update their password anytime under My Profile → Change Password.
